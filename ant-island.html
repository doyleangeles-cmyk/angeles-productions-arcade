🏠 Underground

Your hand with tweezers is the cursor: pick an ADD tool and the tweezers hold it — tap the ground to place it. Pick 🤏 Tweezers to gently pick things up (even ants — hold one too long and it wriggles free!), then tap the ground to set it down or tap the 🗑️ bin to throw it away. Drag to look around the big world, scroll or pinch to zoom — a rival red colony lives about 5 feet east. Sound is the island itself now: 🌊 gentle waves on the same slow cycle as the foam, 🌬️ wind in the grass, 🐦 birdsong by day and 🦗 crickets in the evening, soft rain, and underground a deep earthy hush — plus quiet wooden ticks and thocks as the tweezers work. Real ants do squeak (stridulate — rubbing a tiny file on the waist), and you may still hear that faint scratch, rarely and softly, when one is grabbed or alarmed. Every layer has its own switch and slider in ⚙️ SETTINGS, with a master volume. Underground, tap with a food tool armed to feed a chamber directly, or with tweezers to take out the trash. New: 🔍 Inspect any ant to learn her name and watch her decide; mutate her in 🧪 The Lab (max 3); drop 🪲 beetles, 🪱 worms, 🐛 aphids (on the green plant), 🪳 centipedes and 🦋 butterflies; speed now goes to 10×. No two nests are alike — black ants dig deep carpenter-ant halls, red ants sprawl shallow fire-ant style with two doors. Watch the hole: every ant climbs IN and OUT of the exact doorway — food is carried down the shaft to the store pile, dirt pellets are carried out and placed on the mound, and eggs are carried from the queen to the brood room before they hatch into pale callows. ✨ Cinematic graphics (toolbar) power the compound-eye ANT VIEW. Big, fed colonies grow winged queens and males for a ☀️ nuptial flight — and rival colonies can slide from tension into war: recruitment trails, a shifting front, brood raids, truces, and a chronicle that remembers it all. 🌦️ Nature (WORLD group, on unless you switch it off): wild bugs wander in on their own at random moments — worms, beetles, aphids, butterflies, ladybugs, slugs, live flies, now and then a centipede, rarely a spider. Nature brings bugs only, never food — every snack out there is one you dropped: 🍞 bread crumbs, 🧀 a Cheeto (watch for orange dust on carriers' jaws), 🍬 sugar (pure energy, the RUSH, and a recruiting frenzy), 🍥 marshmallow (huge energy, but sticky — they have to pull). The side dock: every control lives in the slim rail on the left — ⏸ pause, ⏩ speed, 👁️ pheromone view and 🏠 nest jump sit right on it; ➕ ADD, 🪲 DROP A BUG, 🌍 WORLD, 🔬 LAB / TOOLS and ⚙️ SETTINGS open flyout panels (tap again or tap away to close them), and ↺ Reset lives in ⚙️ SETTINGS. Nothing covers the island or the nest. 🏝️ Lily's Ants: the garden is an island now — Scott Island, ringed by the Willard Ocean. A rocky cliff rings the rim, and beyond it the ocean, with slow waves and a foam edge that breathes against the cliff. Foragers turn back at the rim (nothing falls in), crawlers arrive at the shore, and fliers — butterflies, ladybugs, live flies, flight-day queens — cross the water where you can see them. Zoom right out to see the whole island. Underground: the nests NEVER stop growing now — new rooms are dug by need (brood pressure, full stores, crowding), brood is sorted into egg, larva and pupa rooms, larvae ride up and down with the day's temperature, pellets change hands at staging points, and wide halls stand on soil pillars. 🏝️ NEW — Title, species & special foods: the game opens on a title screen (▶ CONTINUE when a garden exists, 🆕 NEW ISLAND for a fresh start). A new island opens the ANT SELECT screen: pick up to 3 species of 8 — 🖤 Black Garden (all-rounder), ❤️ Red Fire (swarm + sting), 💛 Yellow Crazy (long-legged zigzag sprinters, no majors, formic-acid spray, farm 🐛 mealybugs), 🦷 Trap-Jaw (majors SNAP-kill worms in one bite and snap-JUMP out of a spider's grab), 🍯 Honeypot (living sugar jars — repletes hang in the store room, swelling with her sugar, and raiders can drain one!), 🍃 Leafcutter (cuts leaves at the plant and carries them home like umbrellas to feed a fungus garden — no leaves, no food), 🛡️ Turtle (disc-head soldiers PLUG the doors in a war; collects 🌸 pollen), 🧶 Weaver (lives IN THE PLANT in a silk-stitched leaf nest; her underground is just a roots hollow).
