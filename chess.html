Star Wars Chess

A long time ago on a chessboard far, far away… the same real chess underneath, but every piece is now a living fighter from the galaxy: jointed legs with knees, arms with elbows, armoured bodies, and a glowing lightsaber — or a blaster — in hand. Light Side in white and blue against the Dark Side in black and red. They march one square at a time — backs to you as they walk away up the board — and when they reach an enemy, they turn, ignite, and duel right there on his square until he falls. Reading the board: every fighter wears a classic chess badge (♟ ♞ ♝ ♜ ♛ ♚) at its base and a name tag underneath — Trooper, Knight, Sage, Droid, Champion, Lord — so its chess job is clear at a glance, front, back, or sideways.

Battle presentation

Fighters march with a true stride — knees lifting as each leg swings through, hips and shoulders counter-rolling, heavier fighters lumbering slower — and duels play out in three exchanges right on the capture square: strike, parry, riposte, then the killing blow, sparks flying while both forces watch. Turn duels off if you want captures to resolve instantly.
