HOW TO PLAY

  • Run: ← → or A D  ·  Climb ladders: ↑ ↓ or W S when standing at a ladder
  • Jump: Space or Z — by itself for a straight-up hop, with a direction held for the long running jump. Once you jump, the arc is fixed: no mid-air steering, just like the cabinet.
  • Hammer: leap into a floating hammer to grab it. It swings forward / overhead on its own (twice a second) and smashes what the head touches. While you hold it you cannot jump or climb, it lasts about 10–11 seconds, and the head flashes when it's about half gone.
  • Don't fall far: dropping farther than about your own height is fatal — use the ladders. Touching a barrel, fire enemy, cement tub, spring, or the oil drum's top is fatal too.
  • Bonus is your timer: the BONUS counter drains as you play. Whatever is left when you clear a board is added to your score. If it runs out, you lose a life.
  • Extra life at 7,000 points. You start with 3 lives.

The famous barrel trick

Barrels rolling above you can be steered: when a barrel crosses the top of a ladder, your joystick direction and position can pull it down that ladder — 25% of the time at internal difficulty 1, 50% at 2–3, and 75% at 4–5. Use it to bunch barrels for a 300/500-point group jump… or to drop one on your own head if you're careless. Internal difficulty starts each level equal to the level number (max 5) and creeps up about every 34 seconds you spend on a board.

THE FOUR BOARDS — US ROTATION

Every level starts on Barrels and ends on Rivets. L1: Barrels → Rivets · L2: + Elevators · L3: + Conveyors · L4: Barrels twice · L5 on: the full six-screen loop. Between levels: the “HOW HIGH CAN YOU GET?” ladder, with heights in 25m steps (from L4 the labels no longer match the stage type — that's authentic).

25m · BARRELS

Six slanted girder decks, broken and full ladders. Every 8th barrel is blue — if it reaches the oil can at bottom-left it becomes a Fireball (max 5). Reach Pauline's platform at the top. Two hammers, no items. The Bonus ticks down with each barrel Kong releases.

50m · CONVEYORS

The cement factory the NES port cut entirely. Belts carry you and the cement tubs; the full-width bottom belt reverses when Kong, riding the top belt, reaches the far left. The two top ladders retract — finish the climb only while they're fully extended. Fireballs spawn from the central oil drum, toward your side. Just reach Kong's top belt — he's harmless here.

75m · ELEVATORS

Left elevator goes up, right goes down — ride the little platforms, mind the pulleys (touching one is fatal). Springs bounce across Kong's girder: one per Bonus tick (every 100 frames at L2, 80 at L3, 60 at L4+), each alive 180 frames. Two safe spots on that girder are drawn from the code analysis: over the down-elevator and just left of Pauline's ladder. No hammer on this board. Two Fireballs, each trapped on its island.

100m · RIVETS

Don't climb to Pauline — walk or jump over all 8 rivets (2 per floor, 100 pts each) to pull the structure out from under Kong. A removed rivet leaves a gap: you must jump it, and Firefoxes can't cross gaps at all, so cleared sides become traps. Firefoxes spawn from the screen edge opposite you (centre counts as left), max 5. Clear the last rivet and Kong falls head-first.

LEVEL 22 — THE KILL SCREEN

Reach Level 22 and the original's arithmetic breaks, faithfully: the Bonus start is computed as 10 × level + 40 in hundreds, capped and stored in one byte — at L22 that's 260, which overflows to 4 → a Bonus of just 400. You get roughly six seconds on the Barrels board before the game takes the life. Screen 117 of a US run. Almost nobody sees it honestly — it's here if you do.

Honest gaps — tuned by feel, not copied

A few numbers no solid source publishes, so they were tuned by playtesting against the documented cadences and flagged in the study: exact run and jump distances in pixels (the run cadence — moving on 2 of every 3 frames — and the climb cadence of 2px per 5 frames are implemented as documented), the exact fall-kill pixel threshold (implemented as “farther than about his own height”), exact per-level barrel release intervals beyond “about every 2 seconds, faster as levels rise”, elevator and conveyor speeds (“constant rate”), fire-enemy spawn intervals on 50m/100m (“every few seconds”), and the cement-tub spawn interval. Hammer duration sources conflict (5–7s vs ~11s vs 10–20s); this build uses the study's recommended ~10–11s with the halfway flash. One DIP listing marks 10,000 as the extra-life default; the weight of evidence (MAME, StrategyWiki, tournament standard) says 7,000, so 7,000 is what's implemented. Note: the free80sarcade page you linked actually runs the NES port, which cuts the 50m board entirely — this build follows the arcade original instead.
