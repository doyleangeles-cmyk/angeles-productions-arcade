HOW TO PLAY

Up to 10 players — any mix of humans (hotseat) and computer opponents. Set each slot in the setup panel, then BUY UP and fight. First shop comes before Round 1, just like the original. Give two or more players the same Team letter and they fight as one side: a round is never over until only one side — one lone tank, or one whole team — is left standing.

CONTROLS & FIELD NOTES

Angle & power are numbers you dial in — type them, step them with the arrows, hold ←/→ and ↑/↓ · power now runs all the way to 500 · hold SHIFT + ←/→ to slide the view across the wide field · hold SHIFT + ↓ to stretch the view out — it snaps back when you let go · the moving dotted arc traces the first half of your shot before you fire · the camera follows your shot to its target · weather bombs are in the shop — sand, lightning, snow, fog and rain all bend the battle · the Ender Missile teleports your tank to wherever it lands · Q/E weapon · A/D drive (uses fuel) · B battery · S shield · SPACE fire · drag on the field to aim
