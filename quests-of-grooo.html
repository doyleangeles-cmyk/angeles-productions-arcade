HOW TO PLAY
🖱️ Click or hold the ground to move — your hero walks there by himself, never drag him.
⚔️ Click an enemy to attack it; keep holding on it to keep attacking. Right-click = HEAVY attack: a stamina-costing cleave for melee heroes, a 💥 blast for ranged ones (~1s cooldown). Binn, Marisoline & Butterscotch heroes attack from a distance.
⌨️ WASD also moves • Q E R F skills • 1–4 potions • Space dodge • C / I character & inventory.
👆 On touch: tap or hold to move, tap an enemy to attack it, or use the ATK button.
🗺️ Big lands, real walls: every land is split into named sections divided by stone walls with doorway gaps. Clicking far away makes your hero pathfind around walls automatically — try a different route if a section is guarded!
📜 Missions: follow the step list in the quest box (top-left) — beat baddies, recover the lost relics, clear the portal dungeon, then the Exit Portal unlocks.
🌀 Portals: step into the swirling portal in the middle section to drop into a basement / cave system below the land. Beat its boss, grab its treasure, and take the return portal back up.
📜 Quest log: press J or the Minimize button to collapse it to a small bar — your mission progress is never lost. Press J again to expand it.
👥 2 PLAYER (couch co-op): Player 1 — WASD + mouse. Player 2 drives the companion — Arrow keys move, hold , (comma) to attack, walk over loot to grab it. The companion still heals and revives on its own. Toggle it on the title screen, the P2 button up top, or the pause menu.
🎭 Companion playstyle: press Y or the Style button to switch your companion between Fighter, Tank, Healer and Looter — any time, instantly.
⭐ Leveling: kills, relics and mission steps grant XP. Watch the big purple LEVEL badge and XP bar in the HUD — level-ups heal you fully and grant +5 stat points & +1 skill point to spend in the Character panel [C].
🤖 Your chosen AI companion: the companion you picked on the title screen is a second hero who follows you, pathfinds around walls, fights baddies on their own, casts skills, grabs nearby loot for you, heals you when you're hurt — and can even revive you once in a while! Press T or the Companion button to send them away / call them back. They never block your path.
