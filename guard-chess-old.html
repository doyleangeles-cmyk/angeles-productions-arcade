How they move & fight

Everyone marches with a true stride — knees lifting, hips and shoulders counter-rolling, the Lieutenant and the Orderly lumbering slowest — and captures play out in three exchanges right on the square. Guards: Officers use AVADE hands (palm strike, hammer fist, elbow); Corporals and above draw pepper spray — orange stream, defender clawing at his face and coughing. Nursing: PCTs go hands-on like Officers; Nurses, Charge Nurses, the Manager and the Doctor jab with a syringe — the classic booty shot — and the Orderly swings his IV pole. Turn takedowns off for instant captures.
