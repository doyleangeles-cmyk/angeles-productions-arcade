KABOOM!

The Mad Bomber is loose on the prison wall. Slide your stack of three water buckets under every lit bomb and douse the fuse. One miss — every bomb on screen explodes and you lose the bottom bucket.

How to start: Position your buckets under the Bomber, then press the red paddle button (or tap the screen / press Space). Move with your mouse or finger — like the original paddle, your position is the bucket position. Arrow keys work too.

Faithful to the 1981 original

  • No mid-wave pause — just like the 2600. The breather is the wait for a button press before each group and after a miss.
  • Miss = regression: miss in Group 2 or higher and you restart one group lower, with only half that group to re-clear. Miss in Group 1 and you simply restart Group 1. Manual example: miss in Group 4 → catch 15 in Group 3 → back to Group 4.
  • Extra bucket every 1,000 points — only if you're missing one, never more than 3, and none are banked.
  • The Bomber stays where he stopped. The first bomb of a new group drops from the spot he ended the last group — so line up under him before you press the button.
  • His face tells the story: frown while you catch, grin & giggle when you miss — and at 10,000 points he's impressed (watch for the surprised “O” face; from then on he stops smiling at your misses).
  • Max score 999,999. Clearing Groups 1–6 cleanly lands on exactly 1,000 points. 3,000 points was the score for Activision's “Bucket Brigade” patch.
  • No music — the 2600 original had none. Only fuse fizzle, the splash on a catch, and the chained ka-ka-kaboom on a miss. (The 1812 Overture belongs to the later 5200 / 8-bit version — not this one.)

Per-group pixel/frame speeds were never published — only the Group 8 top rate (≈13 bombs/sec) is documented — so the speeds for Groups 1–7 here are tuned by feel to the original's ramp, as any recreation must.
